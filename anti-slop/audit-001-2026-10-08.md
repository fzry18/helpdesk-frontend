## Numbered Findings List

### [01] C-2 & R-26: Pagination controls missing on tickets list
- File: `src/app/(dashboard)/tickets/page.tsx:252-258`
- Priority: HIGH (Functional Gate)
- Description: The tickets view displays total pages and count ("Menampilkan halaman 1 dari 4 (69 tiket)"), but contains no navigation buttons (Previous, Next, or page jump). Users cannot view tickets beyond page 1 when ticket count exceeds 20.
- Proposed Action: Add functional previous and next pagination buttons bound to the `page` query parameter.

### [02] R-02: Em dash character in user-facing text

- Priority: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- Description: User-facing text contains an em dash: `Tiket Baru — Pilih Tim Penanganan`.

### [03] R-02: Em dash character in code comments

### [04] R-04 & antislop-ui: Decorative emojis in notification toast titles
